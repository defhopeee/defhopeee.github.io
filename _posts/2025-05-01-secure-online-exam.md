**Summary:** Exam platform with lockdown browser features to reduce cheating.

**Tech Stack:** PHP, JavaScript, MySQL

**Key Features:**
- Timed exams and question randomization
- Lockdown mode to disable copy/paste/navigation
- Results analytics dashboard

**Impact:** Lockdown features contributed to a 75% drop in detected cheating attempts.

**Lessons Learned:** Combine client-side controls with server-side validation for security.
