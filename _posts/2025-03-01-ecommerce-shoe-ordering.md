**Summary:** Built a scalable e-commerce platform focused on shoe orders, integrated M-Pesa STK payment.

**Tech Stack:** PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, M-Pesa STK API

**Key Features:**
- Product catalog, cart, and checkout
- M-Pesa STK payment integration
- Admin dashboard for order management

**Impact:** Handled 150+ orders/month during pilot run.

**Lessons Learned:** Payment integration details (callback handling, security) and reliable form validation are critical for real-world e-commerce.
